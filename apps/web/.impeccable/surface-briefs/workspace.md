# DeltaX workspace

Mode: Operate

Audience: engineering students working independently or with instructor supervision.
Job: author, validate, save, simulate, observe, and safely stop four-axis delta-robot programs.
Constraints: desktop-first; simulation only; preserve Phase 1 protocol; no copied vendor assets; WCAG AA.

## Direction contract

THESIS: DeltaX is a commissioning console where safety and live state frame the work continuously; it refuses a promotional dashboard or decorative card grid.

OWN-WORLD: White instrument surfaces sit inside navy structural rails. Blue carries telemetry, orange marks primary and active states, amber and red are reserved for safety. Thin rules, restrained corners, tabular numbers, and literal state labels define components.

STORY: User authenticates, opens a project, selects a simulator, edits and validates, explicitly unlocks simulation, runs or jogs, watches command and pose state, then saves or restores a revision.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A 56px status bar and collapsible 72/224px rail frame one large working surface. Editor or twin owns most area; compact inspector and contextual action strip support it. Project, connection, lock, speed, Stop, faults, and profile remain visible.

FORM: Calibration-bench commissioning worksheet, seventh grounded direction from seed `3173e59a`; tab extent strengthens navigation, persistent framing strengthens state awareness, and active-command cadence makes execution legible.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
